Privacy Policy

Promptlyn · Last updated 17 August 2026

Promptlyn has no accounts and asks for nothing about you. There is no sign-up, no email, no profile. This page describes the small amount of data the app does handle and why.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, or postal address.
  • No contacts, photos, files, location, or microphone access.
  • No advertising identifier. The app has no ads and does not use the Android Advertising ID; the permission is explicitly removed from the build.
  • Nothing that identifies you personally, and nothing that follows you to other apps or websites.

What stays only on your phone

These never leave your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data:

  • Prompts you save to Collections.
  • Prompts you have recently opened.
  • Your recent search terms.
  • Your light/dark theme choice.

What the app sends to our server

An anonymous installation ID

On first launch the app generates a random identifier and stores it on the device. It is sent with requests to our API so we can count how many distinct people use the app rather than how many requests arrive.

It is not a device identifier. It is random, created by the app, unique to this installation, and unrelated to any hardware or account ID. Reinstalling the app or clearing its data produces a new one, and there is no way to link the old and new values. It cannot be used to identify you, and it means nothing outside Promptlyn.

We use this instead of your IP address on purpose. Counting by IP would be both less accurate and more intrusive — mobile networks share one address across thousands of people, while a single phone's address changes as it moves between towers and Wi-Fi.

Usage events

The app records which parts of the catalogue are used, so we know which prompts are worth adding more of. Each event carries the ID of the prompt or category involved and nothing about you:

EventRecorded when
prompt_revealedYou tap Generate Prompt
prompt_copiedYou copy a prompt
prompt_launchedYou open a prompt in ChatGPT, Gemini, or another tool
prompt_savedYou add or remove a prompt from Collections
prompt_searchYou run a search (the search term is included)
category_openedYou open a category
gallery_image_openedYou open an image in the Gallery

Crash reports

If the app crashes, a report is sent so the fault can be fixed. It contains the technical details of the crash — the error, where in the code it happened, your device model and Android version. It does not contain the contents of your Collections, your searches, or anything you have typed.

We do not sell data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. There are none involved.

Children

Promptlyn is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them. Since the app collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing about a child to collect either.

Your choices

  • Delete everything: uninstall the app, or clear its data in Android Settings. Saved prompts, search history, and the installation ID are all removed, and the ID cannot be reconstructed.
  • Ask us: because nothing we hold is tied to your identity, we cannot look up "your" data on request — there is no name or email to look it up by. If you have a question about any of this, write to us.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected, the date at the top changes with it, and the change is described in the app's release notes.
